Water for Coffee

by Maxwell DASHWOOD and Christopher H. HENDON
Publisher:
Maxwell Dashwood
ISBN:
978-1-3999-8044-9
Pages:
156

Water is one of the few things you have to master if you want to extract good coffee. This book follows that idea in detail: why water matters so much, and how it changes the cup.

The opening chapters are a difficult read, bringing you back to chemistry lessons. But the chapters that follow, through to the end of the book, give a clear account of how to get the best water for your coffee.

Recipes

#04Hectaguda — Araku

Hario V60 NEO, 26 clicks on C40

Ingredients

  • 16g Hectaguda coffee
  • 350ml Montcalm water at 89°C
  • CAFEC Abaca cone paper filter

Method

1. Pour 50ml, bloom for 30s
2. At 0:30, pour to 190ml
3. At 1:00, pour to 290ml
4. At 1:30, pour to 350ml
5. Total brew time: 2:10

#03Hectaguda — Araku

Aeropress, 25 clicks on C40

Ingredients

  • 16g Hectaguda
  • 250ml Volvic water at 93°C
  • 2 classic paper filters

Method

1. Rinse filters
2. Pour 50ml, bloom for 20s and stir
3. Pour 200ml water fast
4. At 0:50, start pressing
5. Press gently until 1:20
6. Dilute with 50ml water

#02Los Lajones Bambu — L'Arbre à Café

Origami, 26 clicks on C40

Ingredients

  • 14g Los Lajones Bambu coffee
  • 250ml Volvic water at 93°C

Method

1. Rinse filter and preheat
2. Pour 50ml water, bloom for 30s
3. Pour 150ml water in circular motions
4. At 2:00, pour remaining water
5. Total brew time: 2:40

#01Moka Bunna Bet — L'Arbre à Café

Aeropress, 14 clicks on C40

Ingredients

  • 14g Moka Bunna Bet
  • 250ml Volvic water at 93°C
  • 2 classic paper filters

Method

1. Rinse filters
2. Pour 50ml, bloom for 20s and stir
3. Pour 200ml water fast
4. At 0:50, start pressing
5. Press gently until 1:20
6. Dilute with 50ml water
